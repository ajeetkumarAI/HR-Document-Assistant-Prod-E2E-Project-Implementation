# Code of Conduct

## Respectful Workplace

The Company has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act. Complaints can be raised by emailing the Internal Committee or through the ethics hotline, and must ideally be filed within 3 months of the incident.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from vendors or clients only up to a value of INR 2,000 per occasion. Gifts above this value must be declared to the Compliance team within 7 days. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts must never be accepted.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, directorship or financial interest in a vendor or competitor in the annual conflict of interest declaration, and immediately whenever a new conflict arises.

## Whistleblower Protection

Concerns about fraud, bribery or unethical conduct can be raised anonymously through the ethics hotline. The Company prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Confidential Information

Employees must not share confidential company or customer information outside the Company, including on social media or with generative AI tools that are not approved by IT Security.
