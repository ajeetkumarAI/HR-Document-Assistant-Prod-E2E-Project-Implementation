# Compensation Bands and Increment Matrix

## Salary Bands (annual CTC, INR)

- Band B1 (Associate): 6,00,000 to 9,00,000
- Band B2 (Senior Associate): 9,00,000 to 15,00,000
- Band B3 (Lead): 15,00,000 to 24,00,000
- Band B4 (Manager): 24,00,000 to 38,00,000
- Band B5 (Senior Manager): 38,00,000 to 55,00,000

## Increment Matrix

Annual increments are based on the performance rating: rating 5 receives 12% to 15%, rating 4 receives 8% to 11%, rating 3 receives 5% to 7%, rating 2 receives 0% to 3% and rating 1 receives no increment.

## Promotion Increase

A promotion to the next band carries an additional increase of 10% to 15% on top of the merit increment, capped at the midpoint of the new band.
