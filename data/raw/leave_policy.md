# Leave Policy

This policy defines the types of leave available to full-time employees, how leave accrues and how to apply for it. It applies to all full-time employees on the India payroll from 1 January 2026.

## Earned Leave

Employees accrue 1.5 days of earned leave (EL) for every completed month of service, which is 18 days per calendar year.

- Earned leave can be used after completing 3 months of service.
- A maximum of 45 days of earned leave can be carried forward to the next year. Any balance above 45 days lapses on 31 December.
- Earned leave of 3 or more consecutive days must be applied for at least 14 days in advance in the HR portal.
- Unused earned leave up to 45 days is encashed at basic salary when an employee leaves the Company.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year, credited on 1 January (pro-rated for joiners).

- Sick leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed.
- Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate uploaded within 5 working days of returning to work.
- Sick leave may be used to care for a dependent family member.

## Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with earned leave and cannot exceed 3 consecutive days.

## Maternity Leave

Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for the third child onwards, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date. Employees must have worked at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.

## Paternity Leave

Employees receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. It can be taken in a maximum of two blocks.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law).

## Leave Without Pay

Leave without pay (LWP) may be approved by the reporting manager and HR business partner only after all earned and casual leave is exhausted. LWP beyond 30 days in a year affects the annual performance bonus eligibility.

## How to Apply

Apply for all leave through the HR portal (Leave > Apply Leave). Your reporting manager must approve the request. Emergency leave may be applied for retrospectively within 3 working days.
