# Performance Review Guidelines for Managers

## Review Cycle

The annual performance review runs from 1 March to 15 April. Managers must complete self-review discussions and submit ratings in the HR portal by 31 March.

## Rating Scale

Ratings use a 5-point scale: 5 Exceptional, 4 Exceeds Expectations, 3 Meets Expectations, 2 Partially Meets, 1 Does Not Meet. Guided distribution: no more than 15% of a team may be rated 5 and at least 5% should be rated 2 or below, subject to calibration.

## Performance Improvement Plan

An employee rated 1 or 2 for the cycle should be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) of 60 days, with written goals and fortnightly check-ins documented in the HR portal. The HR business partner must be consulted before a PIP is issued.

## Calibration

Department calibration meetings are held in the first week of April. Managers must bring written evidence for every rating of 5 or 1.
