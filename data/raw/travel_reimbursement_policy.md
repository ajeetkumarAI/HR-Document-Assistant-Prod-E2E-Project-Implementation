# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk at least 7 days in advance. Flights are booked in economy class for all employees below Director level. Directors and above may book premium economy for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Hotel Limits

Hotel limits per night: Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru) INR 8,000; Tier-2 cities INR 5,500; other locations INR 4,000.

## Per Diem

A daily allowance of INR 1,200 is paid for domestic travel covering meals and local incidentals. For international travel the per diem is USD 60 per day.

## Submitting Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the expense portal within 30 days of the trip ending, with itemised receipts for any expense above INR 500. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next salary cycle.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, upgrades not covered by this policy and lost-baggage replacement above the airline's compensation are not reimbursable.
