# Hybrid and Work From Home Policy

## Hybrid Working Model

The Company follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected to work from the office at least 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the core collaboration days). The remaining days may be worked from home with manager approval.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for hybrid working after completing their 30-day onboarding period. Roles that require physical presence (facilities, lab and front-office roles) are excluded.

## Fully Remote Work

Fully remote arrangements are an exception and require approval from the department head and the HR business partner. They are reviewed every 6 months.

## Work From Home Allowance

Employees on the hybrid model receive a monthly internet allowance of INR 1,500, paid with the monthly salary. A one-time home-office setup reimbursement of up to INR 10,000 is available for an ergonomic chair or monitor, claimable within the first 6 months of joining. Original invoices must be submitted through the expense portal.

## Working From Another City or Country

Working from another city in India for up to 4 weeks per year is allowed with manager approval. Working from outside India requires prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax and immigration implications, and is limited to 15 working days per year.

## Expectations

Employees working from home must be reachable on Teams during core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 IST, use the company VPN, and must not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
